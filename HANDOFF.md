## Decisions worth not re-litigating

1. **This is an umbrella, not a rewrite.** WINNING_FILM is 20,882 words and
   already covers the craft thoroughly. Duplicating it would create two
   sources of truth for the same material. Three cross-links instead.
2. **The 22 acting rules are the centre of the book.** They were buried in a
   PDF build script. Quoted verbatim from version five, credited to the
   document written for Manan Periwal.
3. **Chapter 12 was written before the film is judged**, deliberately. A
   post-mortem written after a result explains the result and is worth less.
   Do not update it with the outcome; write a new chapter instead.
4. **The Strasberg caution is stated as the author's position**, not as
   consensus. Keep that framing.
5. **The animator's name is flagged as unverified.** It came from memory of
   the production, not from STATE.md or HANDOVER_V7.md. CONFIRM BEFORE
   CIRCULATING.
